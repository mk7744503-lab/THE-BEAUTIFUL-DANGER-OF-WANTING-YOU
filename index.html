TAKE YOUR TIME · THERE IS NO RUSH
CHAPTER 01 · THE TRUTH

It started as a joke.

But it didn't stay that way

At first, I teased you.
I complimented you.
I tried to make you smile.

Then I started remembering the little things. Your words. Your habits. Your favourite things. And somewhere between all those conversations, you became someone I genuinely look forward to.

CHAPTER 02 · THE WANT

I want you.

No jokes this time

I want to meet you, Areshey.
Not just imagine it.
Not just talk about it.

I want to sit across from you, hear your laughter without a screen, look into your eyes and make memories that we can actually call ours. I want something real with you.

CHAPTER 03 · A LITTLE JEALOUS

My jealous side.

Something I won't pretend about

Yes, sometimes I get jealous.

The thought of someone else making you blush, getting that special smile, or receiving the attention I wish I could give you…

Well, I won't lie. It gets to me.

Not because I own you. Because I want to become someone special to you too.

A little jealousy. A lot of affection. And always respect for your world.

CHAPTER 04 · THE POSSESSION

I want to be your person.

Not a passing moment

I want to be the person you look for when something good happens.

The person whose messages make you smile. The one who gets to pamper you, tease you, care for you and make you feel wanted.

I want to hold your hand and pull you a little closer. I want to feel that closeness with you.

I want us to choose each other.

CHAPTER 05 · THE VULNERABILITY

The side you haven't seen.

No performance. Just me.

Sometimes I don't know how to put everything I feel into words.

Sometimes I overthink a message. Sometimes I wonder if I've said enough or if you understand what I mean.

But I don't want to hide behind clever lines anymore.

I'm letting you see the real me.

CHAPTER 06 · THE CONFESSION
🥀

This is my heart.

For once, without hiding

Areshey, I like you.

More than my teasing ever revealed. More than my compliments ever explained. More than I have had the courage to say.

I want to know you deeply. I want to meet you. I want to make you feel loved, appreciated and wanted.

I'm not hiding my feelings anymore.

CHAPTER 07 · THE VOICE
♥

Now hear me.

Not a character. Not a story. Ali.

You have read my words.
You have seen my imagination.

But I want you to hear the person behind all of it.

There's something I want to say in my own voice.

A little piece of my heart, in my own voice.

The last page · But not the end
🌹

I don't want to keep writing beautiful stories about you.

I want the chance to live one with you.

I've shown you the side of me that jokes, the side that creates little universes, and now the side that wants you in his real world.

"This time, you haven't just seen what I can create. You've seen what I feel."
